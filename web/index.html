PRIVACY-FIRST IMAGE COMPARISONS

Similarity Lab

Find possible duplicates across folders without changing a single Drive file. Metadata candidates are not confirmed duplicates.

Possible duplicates in Drive

Read-only: matching filenames and exact file byte sizes identify leads. This does not prove identical images or SHA-256 hashes. No original files are downloaded for this scan.

Compare files you choose

Optional and entirely local. Choose up to 24 JPEG, PNG or WebP files, each no larger than 15 MB, 96 MB total. SHA-256 confirms identical bytes; a 64-bit perceptual dHash identifies visually similar previews, which may be false positives. No filenames, images or hashes are uploaded or saved.

All comparisons are read-only. This feature does not delete, move, rename, upload or modify source photos, spreadsheets or local reviews. Clear local comparison to discard temporary previews and computed fingerprints.
